Case Update — Day 3 The missing pillow has been found. And here’s the strange part... It wasn’t damaged. It wasn’t stolen. It wasn’t thrown across the showroom. It had been deliberately placed on one of the mattresses. Investigators recreated the position. The pillow was exactly where a customer’s head would rest during a sleep demonstration. So the culprit wasn’t simply making a mess. They were demonstrating something. Investigators immediately looked at Polly. After all, Polly knows pillows better than anyone. But Barry knows sleep positioning. And Nora knows the showroom. So now investigators have a new question: Was the crime about a product... or about a philosophy of sleep?
