CASE FILE 01

The Case Opens

Read the Clue
Breaking News, Sleep Detectives! Something terrible has happened at Casper. At exactly 3:13 a.m. on October 26, the overnight security system recorded a strange sequence of events. When the morning team arrived, the showroom looked like someone had been very busy while everyone else was sleeping. Investigators discovered: • Three mattresses had been adjusted into different positions. • One Adjustable Base remote was missing. • A Casper pillow had been moved from its display. • A half-finished cup of coffee sat beside a demo bed. • The showroom lights had been turned back on. • And written on the back-room whiteboard: “SLEEP IS OVERRATED.” Nothing was damaged. Nothing appeared to be stolen. So the Sleep Police have one big question: Was someone trying to destroy sleep... or demonstrate something? Six suspects had connections to the showroom that night. Over the next five days, you’ll receive new evidence. Study the clues. Solve the daily detective games. Watch for false leads. And remember: Not every clue is telling you the truth. Your job is to figure out: WHO MURDERED SLEEP? The case is officially open.
