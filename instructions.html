Your Mission

Each weekday, the Sleep Police will release a new piece of the investigation. Read the case clue, solve the interactive detective challenge, and use the evidence you uncover to build your theory.

Important: The game is designed to be solved as a team. Not every clue points to the guilty person. Some evidence is deliberately misleading.

How Each Day Works

1. Read the Case File

Start at the top of the daily page. Read the investigation clue carefully and look for details about suspects, timing, objects, and behavior.

2. Solve the Puzzle

Scroll to the interactive detective challenge. Solve the four clues and find the answers in the word search.

3. Unlock Evidence

Enter your four answers in order. When they are correct, the classified evidence will appear automatically.

4. Build Your Theory

Write down what each new clue proves, what it eliminates, and which suspects become more or less suspicious.

Detective Rules

  • Work together and compare theories.
  • Do not assume the most suspicious person is the culprit.
  • Pay attention to details that prove or disprove an alibi.
  • Keep track of false leads.
  • Do not share the answers to the daily puzzles before everyone has had a chance to solve them.
  • Most importantly: have fun with it.

Your Final Detective Report

At the end of the investigation, your final theory should identify:

Culprit

Who murdered Sleep?

Motive

Why did they do it?

Evidence

What clues prove your theory?

Frame Job

Who was being made to look guilty?

Bonus: What was the culprit actually trying to accomplish?
